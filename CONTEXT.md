# Indépendant Digital

Indépendant Digital helps French organizations reduce strategic dependence on non-European digital providers. It publishes independent guidance, sells educational and advisory products, and makes consented introductions to suitable providers.

## Audience and demand

**Organization**:
A French public body or company evaluating a digital-independence decision.
_Avoid_: Account, buyer

**Decision-maker**:
The person accountable for the decision, usually a DSI, RSSI, CTO, CEO, DG, DGS, or transformation director.
_Avoid_: Persona, target

**Use case**:
A bounded operational need, such as secure videoconferencing, document collaboration, email, identity, hosting, or backup.
_Avoid_: Solution when describing the need

**Trigger**:
An event that makes a project timely, such as a contract renewal, regulatory deadline, security incident, procurement cycle, or cloud migration.
_Avoid_: Pain point

## Recommendations

**Provider**:
A company that supplies one or more digital services assessed by Indépendant Digital.
_Avoid_: Vendor, supplier

**Solution**:
A product or service evaluated for a defined use case.
_Avoid_: Tool when the scope is broader than software

**Ecosystem recommendation**:
A multi-provider architecture in which each solution covers a defined layer or use case.
_Avoid_: Stack recommendation

**Targeted alternative**:
A solution recommended to replace or complement a named incumbent for one defined use case.
_Avoid_: Replacement when functional parity has not been demonstrated

**Commercial partner**:
A non-exclusive provider that may pay Indépendant Digital for a qualified introduction. Commercial status never creates an automatic recommendation.
_Avoid_: Preferred provider, certified partner

**Independent recommendation**:
A conclusion produced from the organization’s needs and the published evaluation method, regardless of commercial relationships.
_Avoid_: Best solution

**Alternatives section**:
A mandatory section on a provider or solution page that presents credible options for materially different needs or constraints.
_Avoid_: Competitor list

**Alternatives page**:
A standalone search landing page created only when French demand, commercial relevance, and sufficient original analysis justify a distinct page.
_Avoid_: Comparison page when no direct comparison is made

## Lead routing

**Diagnostic**:
The independent collection and analysis of an organization’s needs before any provider introduction is proposed.
_Avoid_: Lead form

**Qualified opportunity**:
A project with an identified organization and contact, defined use case, approximate scope, decision role, timing, and explicit provider-introduction consent.
_Avoid_: Lead

**Qualified introduction**:
The purpose-limited transfer of a qualified opportunity to a named provider after explicit consent.
_Avoid_: Lead sale

**Editorial relationship**:
The site’s factual and analytical treatment of a provider. A commercial partner may correct factual errors but cannot control this relationship.
_Avoid_: Partner content unless the content is explicitly sponsored

## Evidence and quality

**Verified claim**:
A factual statement supported by a current primary source, contract, certificate, reproducible test, or documented methodology.
_Avoid_: Proven claim without a source

**Claim scope**:
The exact product, service, infrastructure, jurisdiction, period, and conditions to which a claim applies.
_Avoid_: Certification when only the underlying infrastructure is certified

**Material update**:
A change to a provider, law, qualification, price, product capability, or methodology that can alter a recommendation.
_Avoid_: Refresh

**Manual editorial review**:
A named human review covering accuracy, usefulness, tone, conflicts, citations, and recommendation logic before publication.
_Avoid_: Human-in-the-loop
